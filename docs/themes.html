Guide · Themes

32 looks for one HUD.

Not everyone likes cyan on near-black. Pick a look that suits you, your screen and your room.

How to pick one

Open Configuration, go to THEME and click a look. The whole HUD, the notch accents and the rendered Markdown change at once, and the choice is remembered. Match system uses T.R.I.S.T.A.N. in dark mode and Light in light mode, and follows the switch live. Each theme also greets you in its own voice, for example “Wake up. The code is waiting.” for The Matrix.

About these pictures. They are the real HUD drawn by Tristan --snapshot-themes with the same sample mission in every theme, rendered on 8 November 2026 from version 0.6.2. The folder and the task are made up, and no real mission ran. Pictures are scaled to 1000 pixels wide, so small text is softer than on screen. A unit test checks that every palette keeps its text at 4.5:1 contrast or better on its own backgrounds; that checks the colours, not how the pictures look.
